Common questions

The app understood my phrase wrong

Tap the thing and choose Edit to fix it. On Check and save you can also tap “App got it wrong? Send this phrase”: you see exactly what will be sent and send it from your own email app. These reports help the app understand phrases better.

The microphone does not work

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and turn on Where I Put It. You can always type instead.

How do I save from the Action button?

In the app, open Settings → Action button and follow the steps. You can also say “Remember where in Where I Put It” to Siri, or set it up for Back Tap.

I bought the app but it asks me to buy again

Open Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Family members get the Family purchase through Family Sharing.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my things if I change phones?

Your things are in your iPhone’s backup. Restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup and they come with it.
