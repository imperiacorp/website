Privacy policy

Where I Put It · Last updated 24 November 2026

Your things, places, phrases and photos stay on your iPhone. The app sends only anonymous counts and crash reports, never what you saved or searched for.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The things you save, their places, the phrases you said or typed, and photos of places.
  • Your voice. Speech is recognised on the device; no recording is kept or sent.
  • What you search for.

This data is in your iPhone’s backup, like other app data. We have no server and no copy of it. Deleting a thing in the app, or deleting the app, removes it from your iPhone.

What the app sends

Anonymous usage counts

To learn how to make the app better, it sends counts through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service from Germany. For example: how many things were saved by voice or by typing on a given day, how many searches found something, how many seconds saving took (in ranges such as “3–5 seconds”), and whether onboarding was finished.

  • Never included: names of things, places, phrases, search text, photos or your voice.
  • No advertising identifier and no tracking across apps or websites. TelemetryDeck receives a random identifier for the installation, which it hashes; nothing connects it to you.
  • Turn it off at any time in the app: Settings → Share anonymous usage. When it is off, nothing is sent.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report through Sentry, stored in Sentry’s EU region. A report holds the app and iOS version, the device model and where in the code the problem happened. It never holds your content, screenshots of the screen or your IP address.

What you choose to send

If the app understood a phrase wrong, “App got it wrong? Send this phrase” shows exactly what would be sent: the phrase, what the app understood, your fix, an optional note and the app version. It opens your own email app; nothing is sent unless you send the email. We use these reports only to improve phrase recognition, and delete them on request.

When you write to support, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to answer you.

Purchases

Purchases and Family Sharing are handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because usage counts and crash reports are anonymous, we cannot link them to you or pick out yours. For anything about your data, or to have an email you sent us deleted, write to support@imperiacorp.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date.
