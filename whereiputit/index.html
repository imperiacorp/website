Where I Put It

Say it once: “spare keys, kitchen, left drawer”. Find it months later, even with a typo.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

Save in one phrase

  • Hold the button and say where you put it, or type it.
  • The app splits the phrase into the thing and the place, down to the shelf or the box.
  • Add a photo of the place if you like.
  • Use the Action button, Siri or Back Tap to save without opening the app.

Find it again

  • Results update with every letter.
  • Typos and other words work: “pasport” finds the passport.
  • Moved it? Tap “I moved it” and say the new place.

Private by design

Your things, places, phrases and photos stay on your iPhone. Speech is recognised on the device. No account, no ads, no tracking. Privacy policy

Pricing

The first 20 things are free. To save more, buy the app once: Personal for you, or Family for you and up to five people in your Family Sharing group. Things you already saved always stay searchable.
